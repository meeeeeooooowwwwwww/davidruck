Why these chapters connect

They were never separate interests.

Music led to DJing. DJing led to parties. Parties taught me audience-building. Sales work put me in the room with people who opened media doors. Media work overlapped with sales management and business turnarounds. The chronology makes much more sense when the chapters are linked rather than flattened into a conventional CV.
