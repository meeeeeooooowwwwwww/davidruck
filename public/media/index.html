Media & Culture

Radio, Television, Events & Christchurch Culture

Media and nightlife taught me how audiences form, how attention moves, how culture creates identity and how quickly a message can spread.

PulzarFM 105.7

At Christchurch dance-music station PulzarFM 105.7 I worked as Sales Manager while also presenting late-night rave radio. It connected advertising, business relationships, audience development, nightlife and music culture.

Funk & Fish & Chips

I became involved in repositioning a struggling takeaway as a colourful late-night destination built around strong fast food, arcade games, street art, rave music and a DJ setup. The earthquake ended the business, but the experience reinforced how much identity and positioning matter.

Large rave events

I was involved in organising rave events in Christchurch. One large event, which I remember drawing roughly 3,000 people, later became part of the context behind the worst criminal mistake of my youth. I address that openly rather than pretending it has nothing to do with my story.

ALT TV and Surreal Entertainment

I later worked as a Producer at ALT TV in Auckland and founded Surreal Entertainment, adding television production, events, promotion, suppliers and live audiences to my commercial background.
