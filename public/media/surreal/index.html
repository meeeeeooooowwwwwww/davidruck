Surreal

Independent electronic-music events, built through direct promotion.

Finding the audience

Surreal was an events brand that grew from parties in Nelson into rave promotion in Christchurch. It was built through flyers, word of mouth, relationships and the experience of the events themselves.

What it taught me

Finding the audience was part of the job, not something that happened after everything else was ready. Music, venue, timing and promotion had to make sense together.
