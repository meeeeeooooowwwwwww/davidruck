ALT TV and ALT After Dark

I met ALT TV presenter Jack during my brief stint at Compass Communications. That chance connection turned into producing the station’s late Friday- and Saturday-night shows and rebranding the block as ALT After Dark.

Compass was the connection, not HRV.

I had moved to Auckland for personal reasons and initially took a sales job at Compass Communications ↗. During that short period I met Jack, a presenter at ALT TV. He asked me to take on production of the late Friday and Saturday shows.

I did, and I rebranded the late-night block as ALT After Dark.

The television work then overlapped with HRV.

I left Compass quickly and moved into HRV ↗, but the television work continued. During the week I was immersed in outbound sales, scripting and team performance. On Friday and Saturday nights I was producing alternative music television.

That overlap is important because it is easy to read a career timeline as a neat sequence of jobs. Mine was rarely like that. Sales, media, music and side ventures often ran at the same time.

The wider ALT TV / George FM lineage.

ALT TV was created by Thane Kirby and Ricky Newby. Kirby was also one of the founders of George FM ↗, which placed ALT TV inside the same Auckland alternative and electronic-music culture I had already been drawn towards for years.

A name that returned later

“After Dark” followed me back to radio.

Years later, when I took over Friday and Saturday late nights on PulzarFM in Christchurch, I used After Dark again as a nod to the ALT TV period.
