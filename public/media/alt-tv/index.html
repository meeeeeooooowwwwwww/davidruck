ALT TV / ALT After Dark

Late-night music programming and hands-on production.

The work

After an introduction through Compass, I became involved in ALT TV's Friday and Saturday late-night programming as ALT After Dark. This overlapped with my HRV and gaming-centre work.

Taking the broadcast outside the studio

One project involved a live nightclub broadcast, achieved with producer Terry Mathers' technical help. It was a practical lesson in bringing programming, a venue and broadcast production together.
