Red Vs Blue Gaming Centre

Red Vs Blue did not arrive through some grand business plan. It came through one of my own HRV sales team members, who owned a failed gaming centre he was trying to sell to recover some of his bankruptcy losses.

The opportunity came through HRV.

One of the people on my sales team owned the centre and was trying to get out from under it. That is how I first saw Red Vs Blue.

I took it on and, in my recollection, completely revived the business within about two weeks.

The timing is important because this did not happen after I had neatly finished one chapter and started another. I was still working at HRV and I was also producing ALT After Dark at ALT TV.

The overlap matters

Sales management, television and a distressed gaming business were running at once.

That period is a good example of why a simple CV timeline never tells the whole story. The jobs and ventures linked into one another through people, opportunities and problems that appeared while I was already doing something else.

There is much more to document about how the centre was repositioned, what was changed operationally and what happened after the turnaround. This page is the canonical home for that story as it grows.
