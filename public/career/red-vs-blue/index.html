Red Vs Blue Gaming Centre

Reviving an existing gaming centre with a better environment and a new identity.

A collaborative turnaround

After an introduction through an HRV colleague, I became involved in reviving the former CUBE gaming centre. Network engineer Roger restored the computers and network; my girlfriend and I worked on the premises and the Red Vs Blue identity.

The operating lesson

The technical equipment mattered, but so did the place people walked into. The project brought together technical work, presentation and an understanding of the community using the centre.
