The work

I sold hotel membership and loyalty programmes associated with Hilton and Millennium, Copthorne and Kingsgate. The work developed my approach to qualification, scripting, objection handling and training.

A foundation in direct sales

A telephone conversation gives you little time to establish relevance. Learning to ask the right questions and explain an offer clearly became a foundation for my later telecommunications and call-centre work.
