c. 1991–1998 · Early experience

Hospitality

Preparation, pace and service were my first operating lessons.

The work

My early hospitality experience included Parkroyal, Aikmans Cafe, Mansfield House, Phloyds, Boat Shed Cafe, Rutherford/Miyazu and Le Bon Bolli.

What it taught me

Hospitality makes the connection between preparation and delivery very clear. Timing, consistency and the work behind the scenes determine what a customer experiences.

Those habits carried into sales teams, events and the businesses I later developed.
