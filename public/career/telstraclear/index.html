2006 · Telecommunications

TelstraClear

Telephone sales, a clearer offer and a more repeatable sales process.

The work

At TelstraClear I worked on telephone sales, adapting scripts and training around the way customers made decisions. A no-fixed-term offer was an important part of the approach.

What stayed with me

A good sales result should not depend entirely on one person's confidence. The opening, the questions, the offer and the response to objections can all be understood, taught and improved.

This became a recurring focus in my later call-centre and business-development work.
