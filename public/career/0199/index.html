2011–c. 2016 · Wellington

0199 Directory Assistance

Building a business around a simple connection: people looking for a service, and businesses wanting to be found.

The operation

After the Christchurch earthquake, I moved to Wellington and developed 0199 as an independent directory-assistance operation. It combined a free-to-caller shortcode, business records, VoIP telephony, inbound service and outbound sales.

The earlier directory work and my mother's One Number concept were part of the background. The Wellington operation was a separate stage of that history.

My role

I worked across the proposition, customer acquisition, sales process and operating setup. Getting the service running meant connecting commercial decisions with telecommunications and the practical needs of a call centre.

What followed

The directory relationship opened a wider conversation with business owners. Customers asked for websites, email and hosting, taking the work beyond a listing or a telephone enquiry.

That connection between discovery, customer relationships and digital services continues to shape my work on GRID EATER.
