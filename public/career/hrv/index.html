HRV: the job I nearly rejected because “ventilation” sounded boring.

I reached out to HRV after deciding I wanted out of Compass Communications. I nearly talked myself out of the opportunity during the interview. Instead it became a major sales-management chapter and, indirectly, the route into Red Vs Blue Gaming Centre.

I almost did not take the job.

During the interview I realised the product was home ventilation. My immediate internal reaction was basically: how boring is THAT?

The franchise owners’ executive PA, Mel, was the person who got me over that reaction. She was extremely capable and persuasive. She pushed me to start, told me I would not regret it, and I listened.

She was right.

The appointment itself was the close.

The HRV role gave me another chance to turn raw sales ability into a repeatable system. I became focused on outbound appointment setting, direct-choice scripting, objection handling, pipeline quality and team performance.

The key was understanding that the call did not need to sell an entire ventilation system. The telephone objective was to create enough value and certainty to secure the appointment. The appointment itself was the close. Once that was clear, the script, questions and objections could all be engineered around that single outcome.

That way of thinking had already been forming through Hotel Dynamics Group and TelstraClear. HRV sharpened it further because I was now thinking not just about my own calls, but about what a team could execute consistently.

The wider pattern

One job kept opening doors into completely different worlds.

HRV was a sales role, but the people around it connected directly into other parts of my working life. ALT After Dark was running at the same time, and one member of my sales team eventually became the connection that put a gaming centre in front of me.

How HRV led to Red Vs Blue Gaming Centre.

One of my sales team members owned a failed gaming centre that he was trying to sell in an effort to recover some of his losses after bankruptcy. That is how Red Vs Blue Gaming Centre entered my life.

I took the centre on and, in my recollection, completely revived it within about two weeks, while I was still working at HRV and also producing ALT After Dark.

That overlap says a lot about that period. During the week I was building sales performance. At night and on weekends I was involved with alternative music television. Then a distressed gaming business appeared through someone on my own team and became another operating problem to solve.

The full Red Vs Blue story deserves its own page once I have documented it properly rather than compressing it into a paragraph here.

ALT TV was already running alongside HRV.

I had actually met Jack from ALT TV during my very brief stint at Compass Communications. By the time I was at HRV, the television work was already part of my week. The two careers overlapped rather than happening one after the other.
