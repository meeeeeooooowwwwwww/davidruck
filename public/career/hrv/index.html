c. 2007–2009 · Sales operations

HRV

Appointment-setting, team coaching and the link between the call centre and field sales.

The work

I managed appointment-setting for Auckland East and Central HRV franchise operations. The job involved rebuilding call structure, coaching the team and improving coordination with the people who attended the appointments.

A practical sales system

I used the AIDA framework—attention, interest, desire and action—to give calls a clear structure. The aim was a useful conversation and an appointment that field sales could work with.

The experience strengthened my interest in sales as an operating system: people, process, training and feedback working together.
