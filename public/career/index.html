First move to Auckland · Compass Communications ↗ → HRVI have moved to Auckland twice. The first time was personal: I had fallen madly in love with a woman I met in online chat rooms and moved north to be with her. I took a sales job at Compass Communications, but it was very brief. In my recollection, the top salesperson was making claims on calls that I believed were misleading in order to get Telecom customers to switch, while management celebrated the results. That infuriated me. I thought the product was good enough that we did not need to lie to customers to sell it, so I left quickly and moved into HRV.
