Career & Ventures

My career has crossed very different industries, but most of the work comes back to performance, audiences, customer acquisition, systems and technology.

South Island hospitality · c. 1991–1998Junior and trainee chef roles across respected Christchurch and Nelson restaurants including Le Bon Bolli, Phloyds, Aikmans, Mansfield House, the Boat Shed Cafe and Miyazu at Rutherford Hotel.
Surreal Entertainment / Christchurch rave promotion · youth and restaurant yearsWhile I was still very young and working in restaurants, I began putting on parties in Christchurch. The parties grew, which led me to larger venues, better sound and production equipment and paid admission. I formalised that work as Surreal Entertainment. Media & events →
Hotel Dynamics GroupDirect and telephone sales for hotel membership and loyalty programmes. HDG taught me the mechanics of high-conversion telephone sales: scripts, qualification, objection handling, closing and repeatable systems.
TelstraClear · 2006I was brought in to apply what I had learned at HDG to TelstraClear’s direct-sales environment. The prevailing belief was that key consumer products needed face-to-face door-to-door selling; I demonstrated that they could be sold extremely effectively by phone, with conversion at times approaching 100 percent on suitable prospects. TelstraClear later authorised me to offer broadband without a fixed-term contract, and to my knowledge I made the first sale under what was then New Zealand’s first no-contract broadband offer. How this led toward 0199 →
Pauline Ruck’s directory idea / Dragons’ DenMy mother Pauline Ruck was experimenting with affiliate marketing and had developed a telephone directory concept backed by an online database. Her appearance presenting the idea on Dragons’ Den predates my later Christchurch directory relationships and was the spark that eventually made the directory model click for me. Dragons’ Den clip ↗
Christchurch directory marketingAfter the model clicked, I looked for investors and became involved with a Christchurch online-directory venture associated with Andrew Beaven and Larry Springer and fronted publicly by Simon Barnett. I was contracted to handle marketing and sales.
HRV · AucklandSales-management work focused on outbound performance, scripts, appointment setting, training, pipeline quality and call-centre process.
Red Vs Blue Gaming Centre · West AucklandI took over and operated a large gaming centre combining technology, customer operations, promotion and community.
Mojo Marketing LimitedI formed Mojo Marketing as a vehicle for sales, marketing and increasingly digital commercial work.
PulzarFM 105.7 · ChristchurchSales Manager at Christchurch independent dance station PulzarFM while also presenting late-night rave radio.
Funk & Fish & Chips · ChristchurchI co-owned and helped reposition a struggling takeaway into a distinctive late-night business built around food, street art, arcade games, DJs and rave culture.
ALT TV · AucklandI later worked as a Producer at ALT TV. This was a later television chapter and separate from Surreal Entertainment, which I had founded years earlier in Christchurch.
0199 Directory Assistance · Wellington · from 2011After the earthquake disrupted the Christchurch directory operation and my life in the central city, I relocated first to Wellington and built 0199 around a four-digit number, business data and a VOIP call-centre. Successful business sales were worth about $295 + GST each. Full 0199 origin story →
Real Websites Limited / digital servicesDemand from 0199 customers accelerated my move into websites, email, hosting, domains, SEO and PPC.
