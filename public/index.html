Three connected stories

The records I found, the rooms I built, the audiences I reached.

My mother Pauline raised me on vinyl. At twelve I saw Martyn DJ with two Technics turntables in Bournemouth, then found Nirvana on an unlabelled cassette. Those sounds eventually led me to create entire parties in Nelson, underground events in Christchurch, and later radio and television broadcasts.

“I wanted the sound, the space and the people to become one experience.”

How I think

I’m interested in the bit where an idea has to survive contact with reality.

I like strategy, but I have very little patience for jargon that hides a weak idea. I want to know who wants the thing, why they want it, how we reach them, what has to work underneath it and what breaks when the theory meets an actual customer.

AI has made it possible to explore and build much faster. It has not removed the need for judgement, context, technical specialists or the occasional uncomfortable question.
