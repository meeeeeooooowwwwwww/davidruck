The common thread

Find the real market. Build the system around it.

Sales, radio, live events, directories and technology look unrelated until you reduce them to the same problem: who actually wants this, what are the incumbents misunderstanding, and what happens if you remove the friction?

That approach took me from telephone sales into call-centre systems, from 0199 Directory Assistance into websites and hosting, and now into GRID EATER, business data and AI-assisted infrastructure.
