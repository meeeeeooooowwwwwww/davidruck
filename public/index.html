The common thread

Finding the market. Building the system. Challenging the middleman.

The industries changed, but the problem rarely did: find out what people actually need, understand where the existing system is failing them, then build a better route between the customer and the result.

That approach took me from telephone sales into call-centre systems, from 0199 Directory Assistance into websites and hosting, and now into GRID EATER, business data and AI-assisted infrastructure.
