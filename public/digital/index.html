Digital

Digital Marketing, Websites & Infrastructure

My digital career grew directly from customer demand around 0199 and became a long-term focus across websites, hosting, domains, search, PPC, analytics, data and automation.

Commercial websites

I have always viewed websites as commercial infrastructure rather than decoration. Search or advertising creates attention, the site earns trust and produces an enquiry, and the wider sales system converts that enquiry into a customer.

Search and PPC

I have worked with SEO, paid search, affiliate marketing, landing pages, analytics and conversion thinking. Traffic is useful only when it contributes to a business result.

Hosting, email and domains

AI is making basic website production faster and less labour-intensive, but domains, hosting, email, uptime, security and support remain recurring infrastructure. That changes where I think durable value sits.

AI as disruption and leverage

I expect AI to continue compressing traditional web-development and consultancy margins. I would rather use that disruption than complain about it. Today I can prototype and operate systems with AI assistance that once would have required a much larger development team.

My current model is increasingly: business discovery → AI-assisted website → domain → email → hosting → ongoing relationship → measurable acquisition and additional services.

Owning more of the stack

One lesson from both business and politics is that dependence on gatekeepers is a vulnerability. Where practical, I prefer infrastructure and workflows that can be understood, controlled, moved and audited.
