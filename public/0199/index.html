0199 Directory Assistance

The project that changed everything.

0199 began as my attempt to build a telephone directory-assistance competitor. It became the point where sales, telecommunications, call-centre engineering, business data and the emerging digital economy converged.

After the Christchurch earthquake

In 2011, following the Christchurch earthquake, I left Christchurch and began developing a competitor to the established directory-assistance model using the four-digit number 0199.

My Christchurch earthquake footage, 2011 ↗

Building the call-centre system

By then I had moved beyond simply making sales calls. I was designing scripts, training people, managing performance and understanding conversion as an operational system. With 0199 I built a Wellington-based VOIP call-centre operation capable of inbound and outbound activity around the directory-assistance number.

Competing with an entrenched directory model

I was increasingly critical of the economics of traditional Yellow-style directory advertising. From my perspective, many small businesses were paying too much for products they did not fully understand while the web was making alternative forms of discovery cheaper, faster and more measurable.

My sales approach became partly consultative: explain what the old product was doing, explain the alternatives, and give the owner enough information to decide whether the existing spend still made sense.

I regarded that as disruptive because knowledge changed the negotiation. A business owner who understood domains, websites, search, hosting and measurable advertising was harder to lock into an expensive legacy model.

Discovering the value of data

0199 taught me that a directory was not simply a list of phone numbers. Structured names, categories, locations, contacts and relationships were infrastructure. Once organised properly, business data could power discovery, sales, customer service and later digital products.

The first website customer

Customers started asking whether we could provide email, websites and hosting. I expanded the development capability and personally signed the first website customer. That sale changed the direction of the business.

From directory assistance to digital services

A business that needed to be found over the phone also needed to be found online. It needed a domain, professional email, a website, hosting, search visibility and advertising. The directory created the relationship. Digital services expanded it.

Why 0199 still matters to me: GRID EATER is, in many respects, the modern continuation of the same idea: business discovery + data + infrastructure + direct customer relationships, rebuilt with today's technology.
