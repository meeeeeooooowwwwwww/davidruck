Story One

What I personally witnessed

Editorial note: This page records my own recollection of a statement I personally heard. I am deliberately distinguishing what I witnessed myself from anything I was told by somebody else.

During a Christmas Day gathering at my mother's house, my sister, Denise Ruck, stated in front of me and other people present that I had been kidnapped and held at gunpoint because I owed drug money.

I personally heard her say it. This is not something I later heard she might have said, and it is not a second-hand account of what somebody told me Denise believed.

I reject the allegation that I owed drug money. What shocked me was hearing my own sister state that explanation openly in front of me and other members of the family as though it explained what had happened to me.

I do not need to invent motives for her or claim to know every conversation that led to that moment. I can state what I personally witnessed: Denise made that allegation in my presence at my mother's Christmas Day gathering.

That distinction matters to me because I want this site to be an accurate first-person record. Where I know something because I saw or heard it myself, I will say so. Where I only know something second-hand, I will identify it as such.

David Ruck
