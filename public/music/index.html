DJ Ruckus · Drums, DJing & production

Music

Music came before business, and it is still part of who I am.

Finding the sound

I started with choir, moved into drums and rock, then into DJing and electronic music. An unlabelled cassette I discovered when I was young turned out to be Nirvana's Bleach.

DJing gave me a way to create a whole musical experience: finding records, preparing a set and putting it in front of people.

Production

I have also produced music as DJ Ruckus. A drum & bass remix began as an exercise in stretching a vocal into a different genre and became one of the tracks I chose to release.
