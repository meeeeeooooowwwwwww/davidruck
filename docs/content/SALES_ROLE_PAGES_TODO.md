## Writing rule

Each page should focus less on job-title chronology and more on:

- what the sales problem was
- what system existed before
- what David changed
- scripts / language / objection handling developed
- team training or process changes
- measurable or observable result
- what lesson transferred into the next venture

Keep claims specific and first-person where they rely on David's recollection. Avoid inflating unverifiable corporate claims. The point is to show a repeated pattern: **identify the commercial friction, redesign the sales system, find the language that earns trust, train the team, and scale what works.**
